Privacy Policy

SonarScribe — Intelligent Audio Assistant
Publisher: Bavarian Tech Labs
Last updated: June 21, 2026

1. General

SonarScribe ("App", "we") is an offline application for speech recognition, text translation, photo processing, and live subtitles, developed by Bavarian Tech Labs. We respect your privacy and built the App with a privacy-first approach.

2. Your content stays on your device

The content you create or process — voice recordings, audio, transcripts, translations, live subtitles, and photos — is processed and stored exclusively on your device. It is never transmitted to us or to any third party. We have no access to it.

Specifically, all of the following is stored only on your device:

  • Voice recordings (audio files) — in local device storage
  • Transcripts, translations, and subtitles — in the App's local storage on the device
  • Settings and preferences — in local app settings
  • Language models and dictionaries — in the device file system

Audio captured from other apps for live subtitles (see Section 5) is processed in real time on your device and is neither stored nor transmitted.

3. Internet use

An internet connection is used only when initially downloading components:

  • ML Kit translation packs (~30 MB per language pair)
  • Offline speech recognition (ASR) models
  • Diarization and language detection models

During normal operation the App works fully offline. Your speech, text, subtitles, and photos are never sent to external servers. Subtitle translation is also performed offline on your device.

4. Anonymous analytics

The only thing the App may transmit is a single anonymous counter recording that the "Pro" feature was tapped. This counter contains no content, no audio, no text, no photos, and no device identifiers, and is not linked to your identity. It is used solely to gauge interest in paid features. You can disable this transmission in Settings.

5. Device permissions

The App requests the following permissions:

  • Microphone — for recording and speech recognition
  • Storage / Files — for saving audio recordings and uploading files
  • Camera / Gallery — for OCR (text recognition from photos)
  • Display over other apps — to show live subtitles as an overlay on top of other apps
  • Screen / audio capture (MediaProjection) — to capture internal audio playback from other apps (e.g. video) for live subtitles. Android shows a system confirmation dialog before each capture session. This is not used for the microphone.
  • Notifications — for the active-recording indicator in the status bar

None of these permissions are used to collect or transmit your content. Captured audio for subtitles is processed locally and discarded.

6. Children

The App is not intended for children under 13 and does not collect information about children.

7. Policy changes

For material changes to this policy, we will update the date in the document header. Continued use of the App constitutes acceptance of the current version of the policy.
