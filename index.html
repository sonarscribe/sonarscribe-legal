1. General

SonarScribe ("App", "we") is an offline application for speech recognition, text translation, photo processing, and live subtitles, developed by Bavarian Tech Labs. We respect your privacy and built the App with a privacy-first approach.

2. Your content stays on your device

The content you create or process вЂ” voice recordings, audio, transcripts, translations, live subtitles, and photos вЂ” is processed and stored exclusively on your device. It is never transmitted to us or to any third party. We have no access to it.

Specifically, all of the following is stored only on your device:

  • Voice recordings (audio files) вЂ” in local device storage
  • Transcripts, translations, and subtitles вЂ” in the App's local storage on the device
  • Settings and preferences вЂ” in local app settings
  • Language models and dictionaries вЂ” in the device file system

Audio captured from other apps for live subtitles (see Section 5) is processed in real time on your device and is neither stored nor transmitted.

3. Internet use

An internet connection is used only when initially downloading components:

  • ML Kit translation packs (~30 MB per language pair)
  • Offline speech recognition (ASR) models
  • Diarization and language detection models

During normal operation the App works fully offline. Your speech, text, subtitles, and photos are never sent to external servers. Subtitle translation is also performed offline on your device.

4. Anonymous analytics

The only thing the App may transmit is a single anonymous counter recording that the "Pro" feature was tapped. This counter contains no content, no audio, no text, no photos, and no device identifiers, and is not linked to your identity. It is used solely to gauge interest in paid features. It is transmitted over an encrypted (HTTPS) connection, and these anonymous counters are automatically deleted within 90 days. You can disable this transmission in Settings.

5. Device permissions

The App requests the following permissions:

  • Microphone вЂ” for recording and speech recognition
  • Storage / Files вЂ” for saving audio recordings and uploading files
  • Camera / Gallery вЂ” for OCR (text recognition from photos)
  • Display over other apps вЂ” to show live subtitles as an overlay on top of other apps
  • Screen / audio capture (MediaProjection) вЂ” to capture internal audio playback from other apps (e.g. video) for live subtitles. Android shows a system confirmation dialog before each capture session. This is not used for the microphone.
  • Notifications вЂ” for the active-recording indicator in the status bar

None of these permissions are used to collect or transmit your content. Captured audio for subtitles is processed locally and discarded.

The App is not intended for children under 13 and does not collect information about children.

8. Data deletion

You can delete all your data directly within the App at any time. Open the App, go to Settings, and tap "Delete all data". This permanently removes from your device all locally stored content, including:

  • Voice recordings and audio files
  • Transcripts, translations, and subtitles
  • App settings and preferences

Because the App stores your content only on your device, deleting it in the App removes it completely. We do not keep copies of your content on any server.

The only information transmitted to us is an anonymous, aggregated counter (a "Pro" feature tap) that is not linked to your identity and contains no personal data or device identifiers. As it is fully anonymous, it cannot be traced back to an individual user. These anonymous counters are automatically deleted within 90 days.
