1. General

SonarScribe ("App", "we") is an offline application for speech recognition, text translation, and live subtitles, developed by Bavarian Tech Labs. We respect your privacy and built the App with a privacy-first approach.

2. Your content stays on your device

The content you create or process — voice recordings, audio, transcripts, translations, live subtitles — is processed and stored exclusively on your device. It is never transmitted to us or to any third party. We have no access to it.

Specifically, all of the following is stored only on your device:

  • Voice recordings (audio files) — in local device storage
  • Transcripts, translations, and subtitles — in the App's local storage on the device
  • Settings and preferences — in local app settings
  • Language models and dictionaries — in the device file system

Audio captured from other apps for live subtitles (see Section 5) is processed in real time on your device and is neither stored nor transmitted.

3. Internet use

An internet connection is used only when initially downloading components:

  • ML Kit translation packs
  • Offline speech recognition (ASR) models
  • Diarization and language detection models

During normal operation the App works fully offline. Your speech, text, and subtitles are never sent to external servers. Subtitle translation is also performed offline on your device.

When you download language packs and models, the download servers — Google (ML Kit translation packs) and Hugging Face (speech recognition, diarization and language detection models) — receive your IP address as part of the connection, as with any download. This is processed by those providers under their own privacy policies. No content of yours is sent.

Text translation is powered by Google ML Kit. Once the language packs are downloaded, translation runs locally on your device; the text you translate is not sent to Google or to any other server.

4. Usage statistics

We do not collect usage statistics or analytics ourselves: the App has no tracking and no third-party analytics SDKs, and we hold no data about you on any server.

The App uses Google ML Kit for on-device translation. ML Kit sends Google limited technical data for diagnostics and usage analytics: device information (manufacturer, model, OS version), app package name and version, performance metrics and error codes, a per-installation identifier that is not intended to identify you or your device, and the selected source and target languages. This data is encrypted in transit and contains none of your content (speech, audio, text or translations). Google ML Kit data disclosure: developers.google.com/ml-kit/android-data-disclosure

5. Device permissions

The App requests the following permissions:

  • Microphone — for recording and speech recognition
  • Storage / Files — for saving audio recordings and uploading files
  • Display over other apps — to show live subtitles as an overlay on top of other apps
  • Screen / audio capture (MediaProjection) — to capture internal audio playback from other apps (e.g. video) for live subtitles. Android shows a system confirmation dialog before each capture session. This is not used for the microphone.
  • Notifications — for the active-recording indicator in the status bar

None of these permissions are used to collect or transmit your content. Captured audio for subtitles is processed locally and discarded.

6. Children

The App is not intended for children under 16 and does not collect information about children.

7. Policy changes

We will update the date in the header when this policy changes.

8. Data deletion

You can delete all your data directly within the App at any time. Open the App, go to Settings, and tap "Delete all data". This permanently removes from your device all locally stored content, including:

  • Voice recordings and audio files
  • Transcripts, translations, and subtitles
  • App settings and preferences

Because the App stores your content only on your device, deleting it in the App removes it completely. We do not keep copies of your content on any server.

For any questions about data deletion, contact: sonarscribe@gmail.com
